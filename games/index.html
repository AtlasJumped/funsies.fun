Family night, reinvented

Instead of watching TV together, sit around a computer, or put it on the big TV with screen mirroring (your kids know how), and build a game together just by saying what you want. Instead of a board game you buy at the store, you can sit down every week and build your own new game, then play it together. How cool is that?

Coming: the Funzies Fun app

Open the app with your family or friends. Choose where you'll play: phones, a computer, the TV, or a mix. Turn on the microphone and everyone talks about the game you want. A first draft arrives in less than ten minutes, on everyone's phone. Try it, say what you like and don't, and it builds the next draft. Play when it feels right, and save it for next time. Role-playing games, trivia, high-score games, and turn-by-turn games that text you when it's your turn.

We don't save your voice. We convert your voice to text, and that text becomes the instructions for the AI.
